    A Sinking Feeling

  • A Sinking Feeling(2026) is a first-person fishing survival horror game. I am the Creative Director and Lead Sound Designer for the game. We worked in a team of six (Group Echo) to develop this game over the course of two semesters at Macquarie University.


    • War of Olympus

  • War of Olympus(2021) is a prototype I created for a 2D mobile strategy game that can be played in the browser, where you play as Zeus, Poseidon, Athena and Demeter to defeat mythical enemies across 28 unique levels and emerge victorious.


    • The Lonely Cactus

  • The Lonely Cactus(2023) is a short 2D adventure game I created that can be played in the browser and follows the story of a cactus outcast who feels isolated and rejected by the flowers in the garden, as it searches for its lost family.


    • Animal Jump

  • Animal Jump(2021) is a 3D platformer I created with a levels game mode featuring 39 levels across three worlds: Grasslands, Forest and Beach. The game features an endless run mode with three themed environments, and contains a shop and inventory system where the player can purchase new animals and vibrant trails. There are also seven achievements the player can earn throughout the game.
